# Contributing

## Status

This repository is the published snapshot of the records of ObliviOS. It is published as source only: no builds, images or update service are offered, and no device is supported. Issues are welcome. Pull requests are not accepted yet.

## How a correction works

The documents are authored in a private repository and copied here by a script that rewrites host and path details and checks the result. A fix to a document is therefore made at the source, and the snapshot follows. Open an issue that names the file and the statement; the correction lands in the next snapshot, and you get a first reply within three days.

## What belongs here

Decisions, the threat model, engineering notes, the bill of materials, the source of the website, and the scripts that reproduce a documented check. Build output, signing material and device logs do not belong here.
